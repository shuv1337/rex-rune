# M3.2 — conditional Activity toolbar chip

Implemented on ec0de79 (after #83). Verification: 2026-11-12, Pacific Standard Time.
This is a focused extension of the [Activity list](M3.2-activity-list.md), not full M3.2 parity.

## Shipped

- A button only while known, unmuted tabs **off screen** report OSC 7501 Blocked or Error.
  Blocked wins over Error; the count is tabs, not programs or panes. All blocked kinds count.
  The currently displayed host/session/window is excluded even when the native window is
  unfocused. Other tabs in that session still count.
- The connected-host, server-ordered session/window walk is shared with the Activity list
  in `Workspace::activity_tabs`. Only sessions in the host's session list with a cached
  layout can contribute. Muted tabs and disconnected hosts are omitted there.
  The chip additionally respects `notifications.show_in_tabs`; the list's existing
  ordering, filter, inclusion of the on-screen tab and unseen-Done behavior are unchanged.
  Sidebar session words are unchanged.
- Idle, Working, unseen Done, Bell and OSC 9;4 progress alone do not create a chip.
- Clicking opens the existing `client.activity` list. The first field accepts typing
  immediately; Escape restores terminal input. This is a button role with a full accessible
  label and an effective-keymap tooltip through `shortcut_help`.
- Model wakes drive appearance, count and disappearance. No timer, polling, status query,
  new action, setting or dependency. No host, terminal, ownership or size command is sent by
  drawing or opening it. Seeing the chip/list does not acknowledge a tab or close its notes.
- Always, On hover and Hidden use the same toolbar/floating-status placements as Downloads.
  It does not reserve terminal space. The existing active-tab reveal key includes its
  presence, so the tab is revealed again when it comes or goes.

## Choices

1. **Words and colour:** `1 needs you` / `3 need you` in WARNING; `1 error` /
   `2 errors` in DANGER. Needs-you suppresses the error count, not the list's error rows.
2. **Short form:** `Need 3`; errors retain `2 errors`, already short. Like Downloads,
   it is a word/count chip, not a dot or colour-only counter. The full sentence remains in
   its label and tooltip. The shared policy shortens below 1000 pt or during a pending
   sequence, and temporarily removes both status chips below 1000 pt during a sequence.
3. **Order:** pending sequence → Activity → Downloads, after the tab track. Finish an
   in-progress key gesture first, then things needing the person, then arriving bytes.
   Identity, actions and the minimum 48 pt native drag space keep their room.
4. **Minimum-width accommodation:** the 640 pt test actually exhausted the tab track with
   both status chips and its two arrows. While both chips are present below 1000 pt,
   the arrow slots give way too; wheel/keyboard navigation and active-tab reveal remain.
   Sidebar tabs also use the shared compact policy while Activity is present; without it,
   Downloads retains its old always-full sidebar behavior. There is no change at rest.
5. **Motion:** no new chip animation. The existing floating-status surface and floating
   toolbar retain their SNAPPY springs. Neither pane geometry nor terminal content animates.
6. **Focus:** unlike the download chip, the Activity press must not call `tx.blur()` after
   opening its text field. Native acceptance caught that doing so lost immediate typing
   despite the headless focus assertion passing. The final press calls `show_activity`
   directly, preserving the new field's focus.

## Coverage is deliberately incomplete

The chip knows exactly the cached inventory the Activity list knows. A session not opened
since connecting may contain a waiting tab whose status this client has never heard about.
**An absent chip is not a guarantee that nothing needs you.** README says this explicitly.
No prefetching or new subscriptions/queries were added.

Deferred: elapsed time, a by-session toggle/grouped count, prefetching other sessions'
status, and activity in/redesign of the identity chip. No always-present eye button.

## Private native acceptance

`scripts/activity-chip-x11.py` runs its own Rex API-13 server, two sessions/four private
PTY reporters, Xvfb/openbox, HOME/XDG directories and session bus without notification
activation. It never uses the person's Rex server, display, bus or notification daemon.
Children are stopped by PID. The two sessions are visited before the baseline so their
layouts/status are known.

Successful run: **2026-11-12 06:17 PST**, `native-06psn9n1/`:

- `result.json`, `before.json`, `one-waiting.json`, `two-waiting.json`,
  `after-click.json`, `after.json`, `after-error.json`;
- original PNGs, enlarged OCR crops and OCR text under `captures/`;
- final run log `evidence/native.log`.

Real OSC 7501 output showed no chip for the displayed blocked tab plus a working background
tab in Primary. Elsewhere's tabs then produced counts **0 → 1 → 2 → 1 → 0** as they blocked
and finished. A real mouse click opened Activity, typing immediately filtered to OtherTask,
and Escape closed it. A later Error report produced `1 error`. At each checkpoint every
session view and every terminal's size/owner/desired-size record equalled the baseline
exactly, including the two terminals owned by other clients.

Not exercised natively: cross-host activity, never-opened-session coverage, Wayland,
notification delivery, screen readers, 640 pt or sidebar/floating toolbar modes. Those
layout/mode/cross-host paths have headless coverage; native acceptance is not claimed
for them. No network-packet trace was taken; the command-channel test covers local silence.
